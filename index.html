3. Welded feeder (khabar pot)

6. Moyla drain system (onek gula block ekshathe)

• Tray slope: samne uchu, pichone nichu. Moyla pichoner gutter e jay, tai samner dik (jekhane tumi kaj koro) poriskar thake.

• Gutter: pratita tier er pichone ekta 110 mm half-pipe, bame uchu ar dane nichu (190 cm e ~4 cm). Shesh e 110 mm vertical stack e 45° Y-junction diye jore.

• Common drain: shob block er stack niche ekta 110 mm ground pipe e (1–2% slope) jore. Duita row pith-pith kore boshale ekta drain duita row share kore. Shuru e cleanout cap, shesh e pit/compost/biogas.

• Flush: gutter er bame uchu matha e hose/valve rakho. Pani chhorle shob moyla stack diye pit e jay, balti lage na.

7. Nest box (bhitore, back-right corner e)

Nest box bhitore, back-right corner e. Kindle er ~3 din age (gestation ~31 din, tai day 28 e) door diye bhitore rakho. Bacchara ~3 shaptah por bairer dike ashe, tokhon box ta ber kore nao. Side e doe er dhukar jonno 15×15 cm entry hole, upore khola lid, floor e chhoto drain hole, bhitore hay/straw bedding.

8. Hay rack (corner V-rack)

Front-right corner e (door er pashe), side wall e weld, chhoto 20 cm lomba V-shape (upor khola, niche choto). Hay upor diye dewa jay, rabbit niche dik diye khay, tai hay nosto kom hoy. Ekta rack = 1 ta cage.

9. Tray rail + removable tray

Tray ta sudhu rail er upore bosano, weld kora na. Samner dike tene bair kora jay, dhuye abar rakha jay. Samne ~90 cm aisle space rakho (tray 78 cm). Shesh e tray ta gutter er upore thake, tai moyla gutter e porbe.

10. Cage card holder

Card ta clear sleeve e, marker diye lekha. Muchle abar likhte pare, tai paper alada bodlano jay. Sleeve sorano jay emon clip diye rakho.

Engineer er notes

• Tier pitch 60 cm: upor er tray niche er cage er chhade lage na, tai moyla niche cage e porbe na.

• Nipple cage er samne: pani line aisle er dike, nipple front door er mesh diye bhitore dhuke. Tai bairer theke check, clean, replace kora jay, cage khulte hoy na.

• Tray samne uchu, pichone nichu. Gutter, stack, drain shob pichone, jate samner dik e kaj kora jay.

• Ground clearance ~ 45 cm, niche gutter ar bucket boshanor jonno.

• Floor mesh 1"×½" rakho jate pa na atke. Mother (doe) cage e nest box alada dite hobe, otar jayga cage er pashe.

• Size NZW/California medium breed er jonno. Boro breed hole 100×70 kore nao, ar pitch 65 cm koro.

• Bangladesh er brishti/humidity: MS tube galvanize ba epoxy paint koro, tahole morcha dhore na.

• Ei drawing concept stage er. Cut korar age nijer mesh/pipe size ar welder er sathe dimension mille nao.
